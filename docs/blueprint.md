# **App Name**: Nur Guidance

## Core Features:

- Homepage Showcase: Displays key information about the Institut, including its mission, values, and a concise 'About Us' section. Includes a banner with quality images and highlights key values, as well as latest news.
- About Us Section: Provides a detailed history, mission, values, and introduces the teaching team with names, photos, and short bios.
- Programs Overview: Presents an overview of the different educational programs, with detailed pages for each level (Maternelle, Primaire, etc.) including descriptions, objectives, subjects, and admission requirements.
- News Blog: A blog section for announcements, events, and news, featuring articles with titles, publication dates, images, and content. Uses a CMS for easy updates.
- Media Gallery: An organized gallery for images and videos, sortable by events, to showcase the Institut’s activities and environment.
- Contact Form: A contact form with fields for name, email, phone, and message, along with direct contact information and an interactive map for directions.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Muted gold (#B8860B), chosen to evoke knowledge, tradition, and respect associated with Islamic values.
- Background color: Light beige (#F5F5DC), a soft, desaturated version of gold, provides a calm and serene backdrop.
- Accent color: Earthy brown (#8B4513), an analogous color that offers contrast while maintaining a sense of stability and warmth.
- Body text: 'Literata' serif, for longer passages. A comfortable and classic choice for large blocks of text.
- Header text: 'Belleza' sans-serif, for titles and section headers.  It combines personality with a style aligned to fashion, art, and design.
- Responsive design that ensures the website is fully accessible and functional on all devices.
- Subtle transition animations for loading content, menu interactions, and form submissions, enhancing user engagement.
